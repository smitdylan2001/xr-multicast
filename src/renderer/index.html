Headsets

Connect your first headset

Headsets are added over USB first so you can verify exactly which devices are allowed to cast. After that they can cast over Wi-Fi.

  1. Enable developer mode / USB debugging on the headset. Brand-specific steps
  2. Plug it in with a USB-C data cable, put it on and accept Allow USB debugging.
  3. Click Verify on the headset in the left panel. Tick Enable Wi-Fi casting to unplug afterwards.
  4. Click Cast (or Cast all) to see the headset's view here, lens-corrected.

Supported: Meta Quest · PICO · Play For Dream / YVR · HTC VIVE Focus / XR Elite · Android XR

Tip: double-click a stream to focus it, press 1–9 to jump to a headset, Esc to return.
